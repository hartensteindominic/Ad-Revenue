# Voxel Vault 3D Collectible Strategy

Voxel Vault should not try to make NFTs fashionable again by copying old NFT-marketplace behavior. The product should make the **3D collectible** useful, discoverable and worth keeping.

## Core positioning

A Voxel Vault collectible is a 3D object that can be:

- discovered in the physical world
- viewed interactively in 3D
- claimed through a wallet-authorized flow
- owned and transferred on-chain
- placed into a personal vault
- traded or offered through the marketplace
- used as a key, badge, access item or hunt artifact when a future experience supports it

The NFT is the portable ownership record. The 3D object and the experience around it are the product.

## Avoid the old NFT trap

Do not lead with floor price, speculative promises, artificial scarcity, fake activity or investment language. Do not make generated quantity itself the value proposition.

Instead, make rarity explain **what is different about the object**, and make provenance explain **why this particular object exists**.

## Collectible loops

### Find
Physical discovery, QR, Bluetooth when supported, NFC when supported, location-aware hunts and creator drops.

### Reveal
Immediate high-quality 3D presentation with traits, provenance, creator and reality basis.

### Claim
Server authorization followed by wallet signature and verified chain settlement.

### Keep
A personal vault that makes owned objects useful even when they are not for sale.

### Trade
Marketplace listings, offers and auctions backed by verified on-chain settlement.

### Return
Collections, hunt trails, creator drops and new object releases should give collectors a reason to come back.

## 3D-first differentiation

Prioritize features that increase the value of the actual object:

1. better GLB/GLTF presentation
2. meaningful traits and provenance
3. creator identity and editions
4. physical discovery context
5. portable ownership
6. utility hooks that use the object inside future experiences

Avoid building generic social features unless they directly strengthen one of these loops.

## Success signal

The strongest signal is not how many NFTs exist. It is whether a person who discovers one wants to inspect it, claim it, keep it, show it, hunt for another, or trade for a different one.
